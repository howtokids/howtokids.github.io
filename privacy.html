Privacy Policy

MY UPLOADER

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers my uploader, a private tool run by How to KIDS for its own YouTube channels (How to KIDS and Optimus Robot News).

What data we access

After you sign in with Google, the app uses the YouTube Data API v3 only to upload videos and manage the owner's own videos (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and privacy status) on the owner's own channels. It does not read your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google data.

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by us. Video files and metadata go directly from that computer to YouTube.

Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use the data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay until the owner deletes them locally or revokes access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

my uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.
